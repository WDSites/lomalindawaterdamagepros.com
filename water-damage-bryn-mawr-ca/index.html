Water Damage Restoration in Bryn Mawr, Loma Linda, CA

Houses and apartments share this part of town. When water spreads, the first steps are the same, and renters have a few extra ones.

Bryn Mawr is an old name that now covers part of Loma Linda. According to the San Bernardino County history post on the community, a settlement called Nahant began between Loma Linda and Redlands in the 1880s, was briefly called Redlands Junction after a railroad depot arrived, and took the name Bryn Mawr, Welsh for big hill. This page covers the first steps and how water damage restoration in Loma Linda works.

Which Part of Bryn Mawr Is in Loma Linda

The county history says portions of historic Bryn Mawr joined Loma Linda when the city incorporated in 1970, and the remaining area was annexed by Loma Linda in 2008. We have found no published boundary, so this page covers only addresses inside the city. Parts of the historic community lie in Redlands or unincorporated areas.

That matters when something fails outside your walls. The City of Loma Linda provides water and sewer service inside the city, so a failed main or a sewer problem is the city's call. If your address is in Redlands homes, the utility is different, and dispatch can help you work out who to phone.

Housing Here and What It Means for Water Damage

A neighborhood data site, NeighborhoodScout, describes the Bryn Mawr area as a mix of small to mid-sized single family houses and apartment complexes, mostly built from 1970 to 1999, with most homes renter occupied. That is a directory description, not a city record, so treat it as a general picture. Census QuickFacts also shows that only 37.9 percent of Loma Linda homes are owner occupied.

We do not claim older buildings leak more. The practical point is the mix. In an apartment, water from one unit can reach the ceiling of the unit below, and who pays depends on the cause, the lease and the policies involved. Tell the property manager in writing the same day, and photograph everything.

First Response Safety Steps

Stop the water

Close the valve at the fixture, or the main valve at the building. In a complex, the shutoff may be outside or managed by the landlord, so call the manager at once.

Mind the electricity

Cut power to wet rooms only if the panel is dry and reachable from dry ground. Otherwise stay out and call the utility or 911.

Photograph first

Take photos and short videos of wet surfaces and damaged items before anything moves, then lift belongings off wet floors.

Treat sewage as hazardous

Keep people and pets away from sewage, do not use fans or a household vacuum on it, and call a crew.

How Extraction and Drying Work

Crews remove standing water with pumps or wet vacuums, take moisture readings in floors and walls, remove materials that cannot be dried safely, and place air movers and dehumidifiers. Readings continue until the structure reaches its drying goal, and the scope and price are explained before work starts.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is a published industry standard for water damage restoration, worth asking any company about. For the details, see emergency water removal, water mitigation and flood damage restoration.

Insurance and Renters

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by a homeowners or renters policy, slow leaks often are not, and flood usually needs separate flood coverage. Renters insurance may cover your belongings even when the building is the landlord's responsibility. Check your policy, ask your insurer, and keep photos, receipts and any written notices.

Bryn Mawr Water Damage Questions

Is Bryn Mawr part of Loma Linda?

Part of it is. The county history says portions joined Loma Linda at incorporation in 1970 and the rest was annexed in 2008, while other parts of the historic community are in Redlands or unincorporated areas. Give dispatch your street address and they will confirm.

Is emergency water removal available at night or on weekends in Bryn Mawr?

Call any hour and dispatch will answer. Whether a crew can come that night depends on who is available, so ask what is open for your address and do what you can to slow the water while you wait.

I rent. Who handles the cleanup?

Tell the landlord or property manager in writing the same day. Who arranges and pays for work depends on the cause and the lease, so ask for their instructions, keep photos and call your renters insurer about your own belongings.

Is my address in a FEMA flood zone?

We have not verified FEMA flood zone status for this area. Check your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, and remember that a standard homeowners or renters policy does not cover flood.

What if water came from the unit above?

Stay out from under any sagging ceiling, shut off power to that room if you can do so safely and call the property manager. A crew can extract water and dry the ceiling and walls while responsibility is sorted out.
