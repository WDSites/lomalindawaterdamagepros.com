Where Citrus Glen and Citrus Trails Are

The City of Loma Linda's planning record for Citrus Trails describes a 112 acre planned community south of Redlands Boulevard, west of California Street and north of Mission Road. The same record lists San Timoteo Wash as the waterway for the site. That is a location note only. We have not verified a FEMA flood zone for any street here, so check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your own address before you decide anything about flood coverage.

The plan covers more than houses. It lists 581 dwelling units, made up of 231 detached homes on lots of at least 7,200 square feet, 125 senior housing units and 225 multifamily units, plus commercial space and about 21.8 acres of parks, trails and open space. How much of it has been built is not something we can confirm, so describe your building to dispatch rather than assuming.

What Water Damage Looks Like in a Newer Home

KB Home's 2018 announcement described 95 homes in seven floor plans, from 1,700 to 2,700 square feet. We have no record of leaks in these homes, and a newer house is not immune. The usual causes are the same everywhere: a washing machine or dishwasher supply line, a water heater tank or fitting, a toilet or sink connection, an upstairs fixture that fails over finished ceilings, or rain getting past a window or roof detail.

In a larger two story floor plan, a leak upstairs can soak the ceiling below before anyone notices. A wet ceiling that sags or stains is a reason to call early, because insulation and drywall hold water far longer than they look like they do.

First Response Safety Steps

Stop the water safely

Turn off the valve at the fixture or appliance, or the main valve at the house. If the leak is on the street side of the meter, call the City of Loma Linda, which provides water and sewer service.

Protect the power

Cut power to wet rooms only if the panel is dry and you can reach it from dry ground. If water is near outlets or the panel, stay out and call the utility or 911.

Photograph and lift

Take photos before anything moves, then lift rugs, shoes and electronics off wet floors. Do not use a household vacuum on standing water.

Call for professional help

Sewage, a ceiling that bulges or water that has spread past one room needs a crew, not towels and fans.

How Extraction and Drying Work

Extraction comes first, using pumps or wet vacuums to remove standing water. Then crews take moisture readings in carpet, pad, baseboards and walls, remove materials that cannot be dried safely, and set air movers and dehumidifiers. Readings are repeated until the structure reaches its drying goal.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is a published industry standard for water damage restoration. Ask any company you call whether it works to it. Related pages explain emergency water removal, water mitigation and water damage cleanup in more detail.

Builder Warranty, Renters and Insurance

If your home is new enough that a builder warranty may apply, ask the builder before repairs whether the failed part is covered, and keep the failed part if you can. Because the plan includes multifamily and senior units, renters should tell the landlord or property manager in writing the same day.

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by a homeowners or renters policy, a slow leak often is not, and flood usually needs separate coverage. Check your policy and ask your insurer. Photos, moisture readings and an itemized scope help describe the loss, but they do not guarantee payment.

Citrus Glen Water Damage Questions

Is Citrus Glen inside Loma Linda?

The City of Loma Linda was the lead agency on the Citrus Trails master plan, and KB Home's 2018 announcement describes Citrus Glen as a Loma Linda community. It is served by the Redlands Unified School District, which does not change the city address. If you are unsure, give dispatch your street address.

Is emergency water removal available at night or on weekends in Citrus Glen?

The dispatch line is answered 24 hours a day. Timing for a crew depends on availability and your address, and dispatch will tell you plainly what is open when you call.

Is my home in a FEMA flood zone?

We have not verified FEMA flood zone status for this neighborhood or any street in it. Look up your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, and ask your insurer, because a standard homeowners policy does not cover flood.

Can a leak in a newer home still cause mold?

Yes. Mold needs moisture, and wet drywall or insulation that stays damp for a day or two can support it. Fix the source, dry the structure quickly and ask about mold remediation if you see spotting or smell a musty odor.

Should I call you or my builder first?

If water is spreading, stop the source and call for water removal first, because time matters for drying. Then notify the builder or your landlord about the cause, and keep photos for both.
