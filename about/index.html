About Loma Linda Water Damage Pros

A local dispatch line for water damage and mold, based on Mountain View Avenue in Loma Linda.

☎ Call (909) 505-3870

What We Do

Loma Linda Water Damage Pros connects homeowners, renters and property managers in Loma Linda and nearby towns with licensed and insured restoration professionals. You call one 24/7 line, describe the damage, and dispatch arranges the crew and explains what to do while you wait.

Our address is 11346 Mountain View Avenue, Loma Linda, CA 92354. We handle water mitigation, flood damage restoration, water damage cleanup, water damage repair, emergency water removal and, as a secondary service, mold remediation.

How We Work

  • Scope and price are explained before work starts, in writing.
  • We do not promise arrival times, because the size of the job and crew availability change by the hour.
  • We say plainly when a problem needs another trade, such as a plumber or a foundation specialist.
  • We use the published ANSI/IICRC S500 and S520 standards as the reference for water damage and mold work, and you can ask any company whether its crews work to them.

What We Do Not Claim

We do not post reviews, star ratings, job counts or awards here, because we would rather show you how the work is done than quote numbers you cannot check. We do not state licence numbers on this site, so ask any contractor for a licence number and check it on the California Contractors State License Board site yourself.

Insurance questions belong with your insurer. We can document the damage, but we do not decide coverage and we do not bill your carrier on your behalf.

Where We Serve

Beyond Loma Linda, the line covers Redlands, San Bernardino, Colton, Grand Terrace and Highland. See the service area page for ZIP codes and local notes, or contact us for the address and hours.
