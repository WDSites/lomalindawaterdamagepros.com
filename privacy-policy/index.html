Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you visit this site or call our line.

Information You Give Us by Phone

This site has no forms. When you call our line, we may collect your name, phone number, address, the details of the damage and your insurer, so we can arrange service and explain the scope. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most sites, our hosting provider may log basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed. We use this to keep the site running and to understand which pages are useful. Embedded Google Maps may set their own cookies under Google's policies.

How We Use Information

We use call information to dispatch service, answer your questions and keep records of work performed. We pass the details needed to arrange the job to the restoration professionals who perform it. We do not sell your personal information.

Your Choices and Contact

You can ask us to correct or delete information we hold about you by emailing contact@lomalindawaterdamagepros.com or writing to 11346 Mountain View Ave, Loma Linda, CA 92354. We may keep records we are required to keep. This policy may change, and the current version is always on this page.
