Carpet Water Damage Restoration in Loma Linda, CA

Wet carpet can sometimes be dried in place and sometimes has to go. The water source and the time since the spill decide which.

☎ Call (909) 505-3870

Carpet water damage restoration is the process of pulling water out of carpet and pad, drying the floor underneath, and judging what can be saved. In Loma Linda, CA, wet carpet usually follows a leaking water heater, an overflowing washer, a failed supply line or a roof leak in the rainy season. Carpet acts like a sponge, and the pad beneath it holds water longest.

The first question is never how wet the carpet looks. It is where the water came from and how long it has been sitting.

Save it or replace it: what the water source decides

The CDC does not use category numbers. It simply lists carpeting, carpet padding and rugs among the items to throw away if they cannot be cleaned and dried quickly, and says to remove drywall and insulation contaminated by sewage or floodwater. In practice that matches the table above. Our water damage cleanup page explains the cleaning side of that decision.

The 24 to 48 hour clock

The CDC aims for cleanup and drying within 24 to 48 hours, and its mold guide says to assume mold growth if the home and contents were not dry in that time. The EPA says things left wet for more than two days usually get moldy, and names carpets and drywall as materials that soak up a lot of water.

That clock is the main reason to call the same day. The EPA also says it may be best to get help from experienced and qualified professionals if you can.

Safety on a wet carpet

  • Stay off wet carpet if lamps, power strips or appliances are plugged in nearby, and unplug them only from dry floor.
  • Do not run a household vacuum on wet carpet, since a standard one is not made for water.
  • Stop the source first, whether that is the main water valve or a shutoff valve at the fixture.
  • If water reached an outlet or the panel, keep out of the room and call an electrician or 911 before dispatch.
  • Put foil or plastic blocks under furniture legs so stain and rust do not transfer to damp carpet.

How wet carpet is dried

The crew takes moisture readings of the carpet, the pad and the subfloor, then extracts with wands and extraction units to pull water out of the fibers. Surface vacuuming is not enough, because most of the water sits in the pad.

If the carpet is worth saving, a corner is released from the tack strips and the carpet is lifted or floated on air movers so air flows underneath. The wet pad comes out, LGR dehumidifiers dry the room, and the subfloor is watched until it reads dry. Antimicrobial treatment goes on where water sat.

What happens at the seams and edges

Lifting carpet means releasing it from the tack strips along the walls, and a stretched carpet needs re-stretching afterward. Seams can open, and a seam that parts may need to be repaired by a flooring installer.

Tack strips can hold water against the baseboard, so they are checked as well. If the wall base stays wet, the crew opens it up and dries the cavity. Wet subfloors are covered in our water mitigation guide.

How long it takes

Drying is usually three to five days, longer for thick pad, wood subfloors or carpet that sat wet. The EPA says drying can run several days to weeks, and that materials should read 15 percent or less on a moisture meter before refinishing.

Ask for the readings and do not let anyone lay new flooring or repaint on a guess. The EPA also says to gently clean visible mold before running fans, so they do not spread it.

Mistakes to avoid with wet carpet

  • Do not wait a few days to see if it dries, because pad and subfloor stay damp long after the top feels dry.
  • Do not cover a damp area with a rug or furniture, which hides the problem and slows drying.
  • Do not steam clean or shampoo carpet that still has water in the pad.
  • Do not hold back from calling because the carpet looks fine, since the surface can feel dry while the pad is soaked.

Insurance and carpet

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, while a slow leak may not be, so check your policy and ask your insurer. Flood is usually excluded from standard homeowners policies and needs separate flood coverage.

FEMA's FloodSmart page lists permanently installed carpeting under building coverage and carpet over wood floors under contents coverage, and says renters can buy contents coverage on its own. How your carpet is installed can change which part of a flood claim it falls under, so tell the adjuster and take photos before anything is thrown away.

What affects the cost of carpet restoration

  • Water category, since contaminated water means carpet and pad are removed instead of dried.
  • Affected area, meaning how many rooms and how far the water traveled under walls.
  • Materials involved, such as carpet quality, pad thickness and whether the subfloor is wood or concrete.
  • Drying days and equipment, which depend on how long the carpet sat wet.
  • Rebuild scope, such as new pad, replacement carpet or reseaming, quoted separately.

Wet carpet questions

Is emergency carpet water damage restoration available at night or on weekends in Loma Linda?

Yes. Dispatch runs 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Call, say what the water was and how much carpet is wet, and you will be told what to do until a crew is arranged.

Can wet carpet always be saved?

No. Carpet from clean water caught early can often be dried in place. Carpet that sat in sewage or floodwater is thrown away, and the crew explains the reason on site.

Why does the pad get thrown away when the carpet looks fine?

The pad soaks up water, dries slowly and is hard to clean. Replacing it is usually safer than trapping moisture under the carpet.

Will the carpet smell or shrink after drying?

A musty smell means moisture remains and the readings should be checked again. Carpet can be re-stretched after it is dried and put back on the tack strips.

I rent. Does my policy cover my rug and furniture?

A renters policy generally covers contents, while the landlord covers the building. Report the leak to the landlord in writing and ask your insurer what applies.
