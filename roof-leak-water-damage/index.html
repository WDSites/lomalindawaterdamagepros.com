Why the stain is rarely under the hole

Water enters at a cracked seal or lifted shingle, runs along a rafter, soaks the attic insulation, and shows on a ceiling several feet away. The brown ring is where the water gave up, not where it started.

So we do not guess from the stain. Moisture meters read wet drywall and framing, and a thermal imaging camera shows damp areas behind paint. Finding the opening from above is the roofer's job.

Safety first while the rain is still falling

Bulging ceilings and wet light fixtures

A bulging ceiling can hold a lot of trapped water and may drop without warning. Water reaching a light fixture, fan or outlet can make the circuit live. If the panel is dry and reachable, switch off the breaker for that room. If you see sparking, smell burning or find the panel wet, step outside and call 911 or the utility before you call us.

How the crew dries a wet ceiling, attic and walls

  1. Extract standing water from floors and carpet with extraction units.
  2. Map the wet area with moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera.
  3. Remove saturated drywall and wet insulation, using containment and a HEPA air scrubber when dust is a concern.
  4. Set air movers and LGR dehumidifiers, with injectidry panels for closed wall cavities.
  5. Take moisture readings every visit and pull equipment only when materials reach their drying goal.

Drying usually takes three to five days, longer for plaster or thick insulation. ANSI/IICRC S500 is a published industry standard to ask about with any company that dries your home. Our water mitigation page covers the first days in more detail.

If you rent, put the leak in writing

The California Attorney General's habitability alert lists walls and roofs that keep out rain and wind among a landlord's basic duties, and counts visible dampness and mold as hazards needing repair. A roof leak in a rental is therefore more than an inconvenience. Message your landlord today in writing and attach dated photos.

Roof leaks and your insurance policy

The California Department of Insurance storm damage tips say rain entering through a storm-created hole in the roof or a broken window may be covered, even though flood is excluded. A worn roof that has dripped for months is usually treated differently, so check your policy and ask your insurer.

The tips add that if repair cost is below your deductible the insurer has no obligation to pay, so get at least one repair estimate before filing. Insurers must also give you a full copy of your policy at no charge within 30 days of a request.

Mistakes that make roof leak damage worse

  • Painting over the stain before the framing above it reads dry.
  • Waiting for a dry week to call, since wet insulation stays damp and keeps spreading.
  • Climbing onto a wet roof to patch it yourself.
  • Throwing out wet materials before photographing them and talking to your adjuster.

Roof leak questions

Is emergency roof leak cleanup available at night or on weekends in Loma Linda?

Yes. Storms do not keep office hours, and dispatch runs 24/7 including holidays. Describe the ceiling and any drip near a light or outlet, and you will be told what to do while a crew is sent.

Does my ceiling have to be cut out?

Sometimes. A ceiling that sags or has been stained for days usually comes down with its soaked insulation. Firm drywall with light staining can often be dried in place. Readings decide it, not appearance.

Can mold start after a roof leak?

Mold needs moisture, and EPA guidance says drying within about 24 to 48 hours helps prevent growth. If a musty smell is already present, see our mold remediation page. Testing questions belong to an independent tester.

Should I wait for the rain to stop before calling?

No. Water already inside keeps spreading through insulation and wall cavities. Call during the storm and start the interior work the same day while the roofer waits for safe weather.

My landlord says the roof is not their problem. What now?

Send the request again in writing with dated photos. If nothing happens, contact local code enforcement or the city building department, and ask a tenant legal aid group before withholding rent.
